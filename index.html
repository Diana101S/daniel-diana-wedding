Getting there

We understand that traveling so far won’t be possible for everyone, but we would be deeply honored to have you with us in Busan as we celebrate our love and commitment! Please don’t hesitate to reach out for help with travel planning—whether flights, hotels, or logistics. We love planning trips and can share plenty of local knowledge—Busan is Diana’s hometown! For those who visit, we hope the trip also offers a chance to experience the beauty of South Korea 🇰🇷.

Step 1: Getting to Busan
✈️ From Incheon (ICN) to Busan
Most international flights land at Incheon International Airport (ICN), about an hour from central Seoul. From there, you can reach Busan by:
  • a short 1 hour flight to Busan Gimhae (PUS)
  • the KTX bullet train (about 2 hours 15 to 40 minutes from Seoul Station)
  • a direct airport limousine bus (about 5 to 5.5 hours)

If you have time, we recommend the KTX train — it’s fast, relaxing, and you can see a bit of the countryside.

Step 2: Getting to the Venue
🚖 By Taxi
If you’re visiting the area for the first time, a taxi is often the easiest and most comfortable option. Uber also works here, making it easy to request a ride directly from the app.
🚌 By Bus
For buses, get off at BEXCO (Museum of Art) stop. It is a 4 to 5 minute walk to the venue. Main lines: 1001, 155, 115-1, 200
🚇 By Subway
The closest subway station is Centum City Station (Line 2). From Exit 13, it is about a 6 minute walk to the venue.
🚗 By Car
Complimentary parking (3 hours) is available for guests arriving by car. If the main parking area is full, staff will guide you to an overflow lot located in the adjacent building.
